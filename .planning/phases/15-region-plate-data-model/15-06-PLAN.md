- **Country enum (RESEARCH Assumption A1):** a closed enum of the Alpine Convention states `AT, CH, DE, FR, IT, LI, MC, SI`, so `ch` or `CHE` fail (a regex would accept `XX`). Reversible: widening an enum is one line. Phase 16's launch regions (at least one in Italy or Austria) fit it.
- **Country display names** (CONTEXT discretion): not added; nothing in Phase 15 shows a country, and Phase 18 (PHOTO-08) owns the display.
- **`PhotoId` regex:** left as is (CONTEXT discretion: widen with the first non-Canon original in Phase 16). The map key reuses it.
