<assumption_delta_decision>
- detector: `assumption-delta scan 15` reported `detected: true` with four `pluralization` signals ("a photo … in another region", "a fixture second plate gets its own prefixed files", "type-check also type-checks", "the no-WebGL fallback"). The real transitions are one terrain sheet to many plates, and one implicit region to a region registry.
- **Plates.** Primary noun: `plate` (`plates[id]` in `data/generated/plates.ts`, keyed by the registry id). Decision: **add-alongside**. `data/generated/terrain.ts` stays byte-identical as the home plate's module (about 40 importers, the `committedMeta()` parse, the 4096-byte caps, the precache input), and `plates.ts` is added for every plate. Rationale: D-15 and D-11 require the Matterhorn bytes and names to stay; rewriting `terrain.ts` into a map would change its bytes and its consumers for no visible gain. Accepted debt: `terrain` duplicates `plates.matterhorn`'s 2D fields; `data/__tests__/plates.test.ts` pins them equal, so drift fails a test. A later promote is forced when Home stops being the Matterhorn ascent, when a non-home plate needs 3D data (heightmaps), or when a consumer must choose a plate at runtime and `terrain` becomes a special case it has to branch around.
- **Regions.** Primary noun: `region` (the registry id in `data/regions/regions.json`, carried on every generated photo). Decision: **promote** (15-06 and 15-11): every photo names its region, and the light table reads the Zermatt series by region instead of "all photos".
- Invariant test (the suggested companion, accepted here): every registry plate has a `plates.ts` entry whose URLs exist under `public/terrain/` and match its prefix, and the home plate's entry equals `terrain.ts`, so a later phase cannot reintroduce the single-sheet assumption silently.
</assumption_delta_decision>
