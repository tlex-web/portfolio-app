## Classification rule (planner decision, CONTEXT discretion)

- **Identity** (convert): the test reads a photo's own data and asserts it shows up correctly (title, alt, slug in an attribute, coordinates, EXIF, caption, a card, a map mark of that photo). Select it with `viewBySlug(SLUGS.<name>)`.
- **Position** (keep): the test's subject is the sequence (the first slide on load, the photo one step later, the last photo, prev/next null at the ends, "Photo 2 of N"). Keep the index, and put one comment at the top of the file saying its index lookups are positions in the series order (DEM ascending, ties by id), not photo identities.
- When one statement mixes both (for example "after one step, the dialog shows photo 2's title"), keep the index: the position decides which photo is expected.
