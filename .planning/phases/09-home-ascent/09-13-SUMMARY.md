### Verdict: HOME-08 is MET

Under Tim's total-keep gate, totalJsTransferBytes is 444,761 B against a ceiling of 468,770 B, a PASS. LCP is 3,112 ms against 4,692 ms, also a PASS. The initial number (180,578 B) and the legacy number (444,761 B) are recorded beside them for information only.

Initial and total differ in the container (they were equal in the local runs) because of when the load event fires. In the container, load fired at about 3.19 s, before the stage armed. The chunk scripts started at 3,335–3,337 ms: three/R3F at 241,953 B, the app chunk at 21,156 B and a 474 B stub. So the "initial" figure depends on idle timing (RESEARCH Pitfall 8), and "total" is the stable figure. That is why Tim's gate uses it.

**Caveat.** The perf "mode" column cannot tell "stayed in 3D" from "declined after reaching 3D". `settledMode` returns on the first settled mode, and the perf run records no ascent-frame-budget or ascent-downgrade mark. The byte and LCP numbers are unaffected, because the chunk is fetched either way. B1 resolves the caveat in 09-14: the perf contexts opt into budgetMeasureOnly, so a perf run cannot decline mid-measurement.

**1. [Rule 1 - Bug, test-only] The CSP e2e counted the off-Vercel analytics MIME refusals as CSP violations**
