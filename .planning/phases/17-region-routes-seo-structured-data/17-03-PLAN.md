## Decisions recorded for the record (no checkpoint)

- D-16 says every block has creator, `copyrightNotice` and `creditText`. The site-wide block meets that through its WebSite node (a CreativeWork). Its Person node is exempt: schema.org defines the three properties on CreativeWork only, so on a Person they would be invalid markup, and schema-dts's `Person` type does not accept them. The exemption is per node, never per block, and `json-ld.test.ts` pins both halves; 17-14 extends the same per-node rule to every block in the rendered pages.
- The WebSite's `copyrightNotice` is the footer line (D-17: the markup never disagrees with what the page prints; the footer line is the one rights text every page under `(site)` prints).
- `lib/__tests__/json-ld.test.ts` (new): helpers `blocksOf(html)`, `keysDeep(value)` reused by 17-13 and 17-14.
- `app/(site)/layout.tsx`: the site-wide `<script type="application/ld+json">`.
